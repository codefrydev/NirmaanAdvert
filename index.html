What's inside

Everything a site needs,
nothing it doesn't.

Built for supervisors on the ground and managers in the office. No cloud subscription, no complicated setup.

Worker Profiles

Add every labourer, mason, and supervisor with role, daily wage, and contact. Find anyone across any site in seconds.

Attendance Tracking

Mark present, half-day, or absent for each worker in one tap. See who's on-site without a phone call.

Goods & Materials

Log every purchase with quantity, unit, and cost. Know what was bought, when, and for how much — per site.

Payment Records

Track what's owed and what's been paid. Paid, partial, unpaid — clear at a glance, no spreadsheet required.

Dashboard & Analytics

Live charts and KPI cards give you the full picture — attendance rate, spend, and pay status across all sites.

Native Desktop App

Enjoy a fast, native experience across Windows, macOS, and Linux. We are currently hard at work bringing the same great experience to mobile!

OneDrive Backup

Never lose your site data. Securely backup your local database straight to your Microsoft OneDrive account.
